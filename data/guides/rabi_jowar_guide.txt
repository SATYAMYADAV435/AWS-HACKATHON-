TITLE: Rabi Sorghum / Jowar (रब्बी ज्वारी) Cultivation Guide
SOURCE: ICAR - Indian Institute of Millets Research (IIMR) Hyderabad & MPKV Rahuri
DATE: 2024-08-20
LINK: https://millets.res.in/sorghum-rabi-package
REGION: Maharashtra Dryland and Irrigated Zones (Solapur, Ahmednagar, Pune, Nashik)
SEASON: Rabi (September - January)

SOIL AND CLIMATE:
Deep black cotton soils (depth > 60 cm) capable of storing receding monsoon moisture. Highly drought-tolerant and climate resilient.

RECOMMENDED VARIETIES:
- Maldandi (M 35-1): Gold standard for grain luster, pearly white color, and sweet nutritious fodder (kadbi).
- Phule Yashodha / Phule Revati: High yielding varieties for medium to deep soils.
- Phule Anuradha: Recommended for shallow to medium soils and moisture-stressed areas.

SOWING TIME & SPACING:
- Optimum sowing: September 15 to October 15 (preferably Hasta Nakshatra). Sowing after mid-October leads to heavy shoot fly infestation.
- Seed Rate: 4 kg per acre.
- Spacing: 45 cm between rows and 15 cm between plants.
- Seed Treatment: Imidacloprid 70 WS @ 5 g/kg seed to protect against shoot fly, followed by Azospirillum @ 250 g/10 kg seed.

WATER MANAGEMENT:
- Deep soil crops often grown on conserved soil moisture.
- If irrigation is available, 1-2 irrigations: 1st at 30-35 days (primordial initiation) and 2nd at 55-60 days (boot leaf / flowering).

FERTILIZER:
- Basal application: 20 kg Nitrogen and 10 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) per acre at sowing time.

ESTIMATED YIELD:
Grain: 8 to 12 quintals per acre; Fodder: 25 to 35 quintals per acre.
