TITLE: Gram / Bengal Gram (Chickpea / हरभरा) Cultivation Package
SOURCE: ICAR - Indian Institute of Pulses Research (IIPR) & MPKV Rahuri
DATE: 2024-09-01
LINK: https://iipr.icar.gov.in/chickpea-production-technology
REGION: Maharashtra (Nashik, Ahmednagar, Marathwada)
SEASON: Rabi (October - February)

SOIL AND CLIMATE:
Well-drained medium to black cotton soils. Sensitive to water stagnation. Drought hardy, well-suited for rainfed residual moisture or 1-2 protective irrigations.

RECOMMENDED VARIETIES:
- Vijay (Phule G-81-1-1): Highly resistant to Fusarium wilt, excellent for drought and irrigated conditions.
- Digvijay: High yielding, medium bold seed.
- Phule Vikram: Suitable for mechanical harvesting, erect plant type.
- JAKI 9218: Widely adaptable, bold seeds.

SOWING SCHEDULE & SPACING:
- Optimum sowing: October 10 to November 10. Late sowing reduces branching and yield.
- Spacing: 30 cm between rows and 10 cm between plants.
- Seed Rate: 25-30 kg/acre for desi varieties (Vijay/Digvijay); 35-40 kg/acre for kabuli.
- Seed Treatment: Trichoderma viride @ 5 g/kg seed + Rhizobium culture @ 250 g/10 kg seed for nitrogen fixation.

WATER MANAGEMENT:
- Typically requires only 1 to 2 irrigations if soil has good residual moisture.
- 1st irrigation: at branching stage (30-35 days after sowing).
- 2nd irrigation: at pod development stage (55-65 days after sowing).
- CRITICAL: Never irrigate during full flowering stage as it leads to flower drop.

FERTILIZER DOSES:
- Apply 10 kg Nitrogen and 20 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) per acre at the time of sowing as basal dose.

ESTIMATED YIELD:
8 to 12 quintals per acre under protective irrigation; 5 to 7 quintals rainfed.
