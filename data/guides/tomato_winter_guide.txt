TITLE: Winter / Rabi Tomato (टोमॅटो) Production Technology
SOURCE: Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth (MPKV) Rahuri & ICAR-IIHR Bengaluru
DATE: 2024-07-28
LINK: https://mpkv.ac.in/extension/tomato-production-practices
REGION: Western Maharashtra (Nashik, Pune, Satara)
SEASON: Rabi / Winter (September - February)

SOIL AND CLIMATE:
Well-drained sandy loam or clay loam with high organic matter, pH 6.0 to 7.0. Sensitive to frost and waterlogging. Moderate temperatures (18-28°C) are ideal for flowering and red fruit lycopene synthesis.

RECOMMENDED VARIETIES / HYBRIDS:
- Phule Raja (determinate, firm fruits, high yielding).
- Abhinav / Shivam / US-3140 (widely grown commercial hybrids with good transport endurance).
- Arka Rakshak (triple disease resistant hybrid: leaf curl, bacterial wilt, early blight).

NURSERY & TRANSPLANTING:
- Sowing in raised bed / pro-tray nursery: September to October.
- Seed Rate: 40-50 g/acre for hybrids; 150 g/acre for open-pollinated.
- Transplanting: 25-30 days after sowing. Spacing: 90 cm between rows and 45-60 cm between plants on raised beds with silver-black mulch.

WATER & FERTIGATION:
- Drip irrigation is strongly advised. Irrigate every 2-3 days maintaining field capacity.
- NPK requirement: 80 kg N, 40 kg P2O5, 40 kg K2O per acre (applied in splits through fertigation).

STAKING & PRUNING:
- Staking with bamboo stakes and GI wire is essential to prevent soil contact, fungal rots, and fruit sunburn.

ESTIMATED YIELD:
180 to 260 quintals per acre under good drip and mulching management.
