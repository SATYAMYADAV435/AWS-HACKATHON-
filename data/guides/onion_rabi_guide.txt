TITLE: Rabi Onion Cultivation Guide (महाराष्ट्र रब्बी कांदा लागवड तंत्रज्ञान)
SOURCE: Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth (MPKV), Rahuri, Extension Bulletin No. 42
DATE: 2024-08-15
LINK: https://mpkv.ac.in/extension/onion-rabi-package
REGION: Western Maharashtra (Nashik, Pune, Ahmednagar)
SEASON: Rabi (October - April)

SOIL AND CLIMATE:
Medium deep, well-drained fertile loam or light black soil with pH 6.5 to 7.5. Avoid heavy waterlogged clay soils. Requires cool weather during vegetative growth (15-25°C) and dry warm weather during bulb maturity (30-35°C).

RECOMMENDED VARIETIES:
- N-2-4-1 (Bhima Kiran, Bhima Shakti, Phule Samarth). Excellent red color and storage quality (5-6 months).
- Bhima Red (moderate storage, high yield).

NURSERY & SOWING SCHEDULE:
- Nursery Sowing: October 15 to November 15.
- Seed Rate: 4 to 5 kg per acre.
- Seed Treatment: Treat seeds with Trichoderma viride @ 5 g/kg or Thiram @ 3 g/kg before sowing to prevent damping off.
- Transplanting: 6 to 7 weeks after nursery sowing (late November to December) on raised beds or flat beds (15 cm x 10 cm spacing).

WATER & IRRIGATION MANAGEMENT:
- Irrigate immediately after transplanting, followed by a light life-irrigation after 3-4 days.
- Subsequent irrigations at 8-10 day intervals in winter and 6-7 day intervals as temperature rises in February-March.
- Stop irrigation 15 days before harvesting to ensure proper curing and bulb keeping quality.

FERTILIZER DOSES:
- Apply 10 tonnes well-decomposed FYM per acre before last ploughing.
- Chemical dosage: 40 kg Nitrogen, 20 kg Phosphorus (P2O5), and 20 kg Potash (K2O) per acre.
- Apply 50% N and 100% P and K at transplanting. Remaining 50% N top-dressed 30 days after transplanting.

ESTIMATED YIELD:
100 to 140 quintals per acre under good management.
