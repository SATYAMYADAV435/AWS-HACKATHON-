TITLE: Safflower (करडई / कुसुम) Production Guide
SOURCE: ICAR - Indian Institute of Oilseeds Research (IIOR) Hyderabad & VNMKV Parbhani
DATE: 2024-08-30
LINK: https://icar-iior.org.in/safflower-production-technology
REGION: Marathwada & Western Maharashtra (Solapur, Ahmednagar, Marathwada)
SEASON: Rabi (September - February)

SOIL AND CLIMATE:
Deep black cotton soils capable of holding moisture. Safflower has a deep taproot system (up to 2-3 meters) and can extract moisture and nutrients from deep subsoil layers. Highly drought tolerant.

RECOMMENDED VARIETIES:
- Bhima: High oil content, spiny, very popular in Maharashtra.
- Phule Kusuma / SSF 658: Non-spiny / spiny, high yielding and tolerant to aphid attacks.
- PBNS 12: High yielding for dryland farming.

SOWING SCHEDULE:
- Optimum sowing window: September 20 to October 10. Late sowing leads to severe aphid infestation during flowering.
- Seed Rate: 4-5 kg per acre.
- Spacing: 45 cm between rows and 20 cm between plants.
- Seed Treatment: Trichoderma @ 4 g/kg seed to prevent seedling blight.

WATER REQUIREMENTS:
- Predominantly grown as a dryland rainfed crop on residual moisture.
- If one irrigation is available, apply at 35-40 days (elongation stage). If two irrigations are possible, apply at elongation and flowering.

FERTILIZER:
- Basal application of 16 kg Nitrogen and 16 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) per acre at sowing time.

ESTIMATED YIELD:
6 to 10 quintals per acre under rainfed conditions; 10 to 14 quintals with 1-2 irrigations.
